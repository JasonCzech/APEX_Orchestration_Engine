**Closed** means the reported defect has a current implementation and the evidence below supports its resolution; it does not certify the whole page or WCAG conformance. **Open** means a concrete residual defect remains. **Not testable here** means the affected surface cannot be exercised in this build; a corrected underlying token is distinguished from a rendered result. Real screen-reader speech, real provider execution, production authentication expiry and the unreachable calendar are not inferred from DOM tests.
